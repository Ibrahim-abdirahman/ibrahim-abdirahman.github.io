Humanitarian Delivery Monitoring Dashboard

Executive Power BI solution designed to simulate humanitarian assistance monitoring, delivery oversight, and performance reporting.

Executive Summary

This Power BI solution demonstrates how humanitarian operations can be monitored through executive dashboards that consolidate delivery indicators into a single decision-support experience. The dashboard simulates operational reporting scenarios commonly encountered during assistance delivery cycles.

Business Challenge

Programme teams require timely visibility into delivery performance, transfer execution, and operational bottlenecks. Traditional reporting methods often provide fragmented information that delays decision-making and reduces operational awareness.

Analytical Approach

  • Data preparation and transformation using Power Query
  • KPI development using DAX calculations
  • Executive dashboard design principles
  • Operational storytelling through visualization
  • Trend and comparative analysis

Dashboard Walkthrough

  • Delivery Overview for monitoring assistance execution
  • Regional comparisons to identify performance differences
  • Reconciliation monitoring indicators
  • Validation and assurance tracking
  • Monthly trend analysis and executive KPIs

Key Insights Demonstrated

  • Rapid identification of delivery bottlenecks
  • Improved operational visibility
  • Monitoring of transfer outcomes
  • Performance tracking across reporting cycles
  • Executive-level decision support

Technologies Used

Power BI
Power Query
DAX
Excel

Lessons Learned

Effective humanitarian reporting requires balancing technical sophistication with simplicity. Executive users benefit most from concise, actionable indicators presented through intuitive visual experiences.

Data Disclaimer

This dashboard utilizes synthetic datasets created solely for demonstration purposes. No real beneficiary records, programme information, or confidential organizational data have been used.
